# Runtime art and lighting

These assets are bundled locally with the game; playing makes no third-party requests.

- **Civilian explorer and researcher, including native idle/walk/run motion:** Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library, MIT, copyright © 2020 Microsoft. Source: https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox/tree/0943055db6ec570bcef9f2c8b41c9e5467c808f9 . Characters `Male_Adult_02` and `Male_Adult_03`; motions `m_idle_neutral_01`, `m_walk_neutral`, and `m_run_neutral`. Local files: `explorer.glb` and `researcher.glb`. The complete license is in [ROCKETBOX-LICENSE.txt](ROCKETBOX-LICENSE.txt). Adaptations: PBR material reconstruction, 1,024-pixel texture limits, common physical scale and forward axis, and native motion retargeted into each character's rest pose with horizontal root travel removed. Rebuild with `blender -b --python tools/build-human-assets.py -- /path/to/Microsoft-Rocketbox assets/realism`. The previous helmeted Mixamo character has been removed from the runtime.
- **Compatibility file for cached earlier releases:** `operative.glb` remains available for previously cached scripts only. It is Mixamo’s Vanguard character from the [Three.js r170 Soldier example](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r170/examples/models/gltf/Soldier.glb), under [Adobe’s Mixamo usage terms](https://helpx.adobe.com/creative-cloud/faq/mixamo-faq.html). The current game loads the two civilian models above.
- **HDR lighting probe:** `royal_esplanade_1k.hdr`, originating from HDRI Haven / Poly Haven's CC0 lighting collection, mirrored in the Three.js r170 examples. Source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r170/examples/textures/equirectangular/royal_esplanade_1k.hdr . Local filename: `institute-light.hdr`.
- **Campus night background:** original generated environmental image created for Math Tower, stored as `campus-night.png`. It is a distant backdrop, not filmed footage.
- **Environment, machinery, robots, creatures and weather:** original procedural geometry authored for Math Tower. Concrete and brushed-metal surface maps are generated locally by the renderer. The human rig is a production asset; the environments remain authored procedural sets, not scanned real buildings.
- **Three.js and add-ons:** r170, MIT. License: `../../vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`.

The game uses its own institute, characters and story. The television reference informed atmosphere and pacing; no television footage, music, likenesses or franchise assets are included.
